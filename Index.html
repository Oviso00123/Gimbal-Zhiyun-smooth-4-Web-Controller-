Smooth 4 User Guide

1. Bluetooth BLE Pairing

• Power on your Zhiyun Smooth 4 by holding down the power button until the LEDs illuminate.
• Tap the "⚡ CONNECT SMOOTH 4" button at the top.
• In the browser prompt, select your Smooth 4 gimbal. The web app automatically attaches the control service 0x0012 with a 55ms refresh loop.

2. Gyroscope & Air Mouse Mode

• Tap the "Gyroscope Air Mouse" toggle to activate real-time motion control.
• On iOS / Safari: Accept the motion sensor permission prompt.
• Hold your phone horizontally: tilting it left/right or up/down steers the gimbal proportionally. Tap OFF at any time to instantly zero-out motor speeds.

3. Motion Routines & MediaPipe Gestures

• Routines: Create and playback timed sequences (e.g., continuous 180° pan for 4 seconds).
• Hand Gestures: Activate your front camera under the Vision AI tab.
- Show an Open Palm to halt the motors immediately.
- Show a Closed Fist to recenter the axes to 2048.
- Raise your Index Finger to steer Pan left or right.

4. J.A.R.V.I.S. Voice Assistant

• Tap "ACTIVATE" on the J.A.R.V.I.S. panel and allow microphone access.
• Speak clear English commands: "Up", "Down", "Left", "Right", "Center", or "Stop".
• Tap the button again anytime to disable the active microphone listener.
